Your workflow
{% if g.user.role == 'policyholder' %}
  1. Open My Policies and confirm your policy is active.
  2. Choose New Claim, select a covered member, hospital and treatment.
  3. Save a draft or submit when the required checklist items are provided.
  4. Track progress in My Claims; respond if more information is requested.
  5. Read the claim timeline to understand each recorded decision.
{% elif g.user.role == 'hospital_desk' %}
  1. Create cashless claims only for your linked network hospital.
  2. Select a valid policy and a member covered by that policy.
  3. Update document checklist metadata and submit the claim.
  4. Monitor the Hospital Claims page for requests and decisions.
{% elif g.user.role == 'claims_officer' %}
  1. Open the Review Queue and select a submitted claim.
  2. Assign the claim to yourself before starting review.
  3. Check coverage and documents; request missing information when needed.
  4. Approve with an eligible amount and reason, or reject with a clear reason.
{% else %}
  1. Use Users & Roles to create accounts and assign staff roles.
  2. Maintain hospitals and fictional insurance plans.
  3. Record settlement only after a claim is approved.
  4. Use Reports to review counts and export the demo report.
{% endif %}
Security reminders
  • Never share your password.
  • Only access records permitted for your role.
  • Staff roles are assigned by an administrator.
  • All information in this project is fictional.
Simulated payout

(Eligible amount − applied deductible) × (1 − co-pay % ÷ 100)

This is a learning example, not an actual insurer decision or payment.
